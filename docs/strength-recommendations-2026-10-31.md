# Experienced strength recommendations — 31 October 2026

## Principles and limits

The goal remains muscle development or retention alongside gradual fat loss and useful strength. ACSM's 2026 overview supports progressive resistance training, with heavier loading particularly useful for maximal strength and hypertrophy possible across loading ranges. Multiple sets and an appropriate weekly stimulus matter. These findings do not establish a unique kilogram prescription for an individual, a machine-to-barbell conversion, or a guaranteed physique outcome. [ACSM position stand](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/), [Currier et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37414459/).

Experience selects a recommendation approach; it does not prove that every exercise needs a large load. Working-set references, actual completed exercise-specific logs, technique and recovery are more useful than copying someone else's weight. The app does not calculate or claim a measured 1RM from a working set, and historical PRs do not prescribe everyday loads.

## Private calibration

Your profile contains a separate training form. Enter a repeatable total barbell load (including the bar), working reps, sets and reference date for bench, squat or conventional deadlift. References support3–12 reps and1–20 sets; they are working references rather than maximal singles. The date defaults to the local day. These values are private account/device data and are not website defaults or source-code constants.

For the initial trial, bench/squat use approximately90% and deadlift85% of the working reference, rounded down to the exercise's load increment. These buffers are practical coaching choices, not measured1RM percentages or a study-validated prediction. The additional initial deadlift buffer suits the existing squat/deadlift session. Begin at the lower end of the exercise's rep guide and adjust according to actual performance.

Programme guides remain bench3×6–8, squat3×4–6 and deadlift2×3–5. Recording a larger working-set capability does not automatically add volume; sets remain editable. Tuesday's emphasis/recovery guidance and optional weekends remain in place.

## Sources and progression

- Starter mode retains the original beginner examples.
- Experienced mode uses an applicable dated calibration or a more recent completed exercise-specific session. Future records/calibrations do not influence past workout dates.
- With no applicable history/calibration, experienced mode displays the practical trials below. Cardio and bodyweight activities keep their own guides; assisted pull-ups initially suggest15–25kg assistance, where less assistance means greater difficulty.
- For fully completed, same-load working sets with an unchanged set count, add at most one suggested rep across the exercise, selecting a lowest-rep set first. The other sets stay steady.
- Once every working set reaches the upper guide, consider the existing smallest equipment-appropriate increment and restart the lower rep target. This is advice; actual logs stay empty until the user enters them.
- Mixed loads, unfinished sets or a changed set count suppress automatic progression. Preserve the individual completed loads. After a fully completed same-load session below the lower rep guide, suggest a lighter trial (approximately10% reduction with equipment rounding) instead of demanding more reps at the same weight.
- Warm-up examples ramp at approximately40/60/80% of the suggested working load, with low rep counts. They remain optional/editable and do not count toward progression. Cardio and mobility repeat comfortable duration/reps rather than escalating recovery work.

## Generic experienced first-session trials

These ranges are starting suggestions, **not experimentally validated individual strength estimates**. The first displayed suggestion is the lower end. A private main-lift calibration supersedes its generic range; subsequent completed sessions supersede the trial. Dumbbell entries are per hand unless marked total; cables are per side when stated. Machine/stack readings require adjustment on the actual equipment because leverages, sleds and pulley ratios vary.

Use the first set to establish a repeatable load. The final reps should require effort while leaving a little capacity and maintaining control. Reduce a trial that prevents reaching the lower rep guide cleanly. No RIR/Form logging is required; heavier weights are not automatically better.

## Compatibility and privacy

The training object is optional in version-1 profiles/backups. Older profiles remain valid and use starter mode until configured. Nutrition-profile saves preserve training settings. The same Firebase UID remains the only account allowed to access records; rules additionally validate the optional nested settings. Already-open older tabs should refresh before using the new fields. Personal values are configured through the private form after deployment, never inserted into the public source.
